## Linux 3D card renderer POC

- Created `omarchy-cards-3d-render` from the committed Omarchy desktop branch.
- Added a Blade/Vulkan card compositor POC for Linux. `--card-proof`
  captures the native GPUI card face into an offscreen GPU texture, then draws
  the perspective face, physical edge, projected shadow, and clear-coat light
  with WGSL. No CPU readback or new dependency is used.
- Face pixels are cached while only the pose changes. Replaced textures remain
  alive until the prior GPU submission completes. Hidden faces expire and all
  live or retired faces share a 64 MiB ceiling.
- The Linux path is enabled by the proof fixture and by Omarchy mode. Cards and
  focused fronts use pointer tilt there. Other Linux modes remain flat, and
  reduced motion disables the spatial interaction.
- This POC supports the primitives used by the real proof card: quads, shadows,
  underlines, text, and image atlas sprites. Nested card surfaces and vector
  paths remain deferred before this can become a general Linux compositor.
