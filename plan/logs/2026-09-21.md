- The pinned renderer has no perspective transform for image/UI subtrees.
  Current effects use native gradients, shadows, and clipping. True perspective,
  holographic foil shaders, and physical tilt remain a dedicated rendering task.
- Table sorting, column controls, and bulk editing remain planned. Grid/card
  view choice is session-only.
- Linux, minimum-window resizing, native reduced-motion interaction, and GPU
  frame-time profiling were not verified in this pass. Existing custom GPUI
